\section{Experimental Protocol}

The dataset contains 3{,}475 chest X-ray images ($299\!\times\!299$\,px) across three classes: Normal (1{,}250, 36.0\%), Opacity (1{,}125, 32.4\%), and Pneumonia (1{,}100, 31.7\%), yielding a mild imbalance ratio of 1.14:1.

A \textbf{stratified 80/20 hold-out} split (\texttt{random\_state=42}) partitions the data into a training pool (2{,}780 images) and a test set (695 images) that is \emph{locked immediately and never accessed until final evaluation}. Stratification preserves class proportions in both splits, preventing near-zero minority representation in small partitions~\cite{raschka2018}. On the 80\% training pool, \textbf{stratified 5-fold cross-validation} produces five train/validation partitions ($\sim$2{,}224 / $\sim$556 per fold), providing a low-variance generalisation estimate critical for only 3{,}475 images. Model selection uses \textbf{mean validation macro-F1} across folds---not accuracy, which is misleading under class imbalance as it is dominated by the majority class~\cite{sokolova2009}. The best configuration is retrained on the full 80\% pool and evaluated \emph{once} on the locked test set~\cite{hastie2009}. All random number generators (Python, NumPy, PyTorch, CUDA) are seeded at 42 with \texttt{cudnn.deterministic=True} for full reproducibility.

\section{Data Loading, Preprocessing, and Augmentation}

\subsection{Deterministic Preprocessing (All Splits)}

All images undergo three fixed operations via Albumentations (Figure~\ref{fig:aug}). First, \textbf{Resize}$(300\!\times\!300)$ matches EfficientNet-B3's native resolution~\cite{tan2019}, incurring negligible distortion from the 299$\to$300\,px adjustment. Second, \textbf{CLAHE} (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalisation, \texttt{clip\_limit=2.0}, $8\!\times\!8$ tiles) enhances local contrast on non-overlapping tiles independently, revealing subtle pulmonary opacities masked by the wide global dynamic range of X-ray imaging---standard since CheXNet~\cite{rajpurkar2017} and validated in recent benchmarks achieving macro-AUC 0.906 on NIH ChestX-ray14~\cite{sawah2026}. Critically, CLAHE operates on \texttt{uint8} intensities \emph{before} normalisation. Third, \textbf{Normalize} applies ImageNet channel-wise statistics ($\mu$=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406], $\sigma$=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]), mandatory for pretrained weights.

\subsection{Stochastic Augmentation (Training Only)}

Five augmentations model clinically realistic variability: \textbf{HorizontalFlip}$(p\!=\!0.5)$ exploits bilateral lung symmetry (vertical flip is \emph{prohibited}---anatomically non-physiological); \textbf{Rotate}$(\pm10°, p\!=\!0.5)$ models patient-positioning variance~\cite{castro2020}; \textbf{RandomBrightnessContrast}$(\pm0.2, p\!=\!0.5)$ simulates X-ray exposure variability across scanners; \textbf{ElasticTransform}$(\alpha\!=\!1, \sigma\!=\!50, p\!=\!0.3)$ models soft-tissue deformation~\cite{garcea2023}; \textbf{GaussNoise}$(var\!\in\![10,50], p\!=\!0.3)$ simulates low-dose scanner noise.

\subsection{Batch-Level Augmentation}

\textbf{MixUp}~\cite{zhang2018} ($\alpha\!=\!0.2$) and \textbf{CutMix}~\cite{yun2019} ($\alpha\!=\!1.0$, validated as optimal for medical images~\cite{mediau2025}) are alternated randomly ($p\!=\!0.5$) per batch. MixUp interpolates images and labels ($\tilde{x} = \lambda x_i + (1\!-\!\lambda) x_j$), smoothing decision boundaries. CutMix pastes rectangular patches between images, forcing recognition from partial views---critical for localised opacity detection. On mixed batches, \texttt{label\_smoothing=0.0} (MixUp already produces soft labels; combining causes over-regularisation). On unmixed batches, $\varepsilon\!=\!0.1$~\cite{szegedy2016}.

\subsection{Class Imbalance Handling}

\textbf{WeightedRandomSampler} (WRS) assigns each sample weight $w_i = 1/\text{count}(\text{class}_i)$ with \texttt{replacement=True}, ensuring approximately equal class representation per batch. Unlike class-weighted loss (which rescales gradient magnitudes but leaves AdamW's moment-estimate covariance dominated by majority-class features), WRS balances gradient covariance in both magnitude and sample diversity~\cite{buda2018}. WRS is rebuilt \emph{per fold} from fold-local labels to prevent data leakage. Loss weights are \emph{not} added to \texttt{CrossEntropyLoss} to avoid double-correction. Training DataLoaders use \texttt{drop\_last=True} to stabilise BatchNorm statistics.

\section{Network Architectures, Strategies, and Training}

\subsection{Custom CNN: LightXRayCNN ($\sim$1.2M parameters)}

The custom network employs \textbf{depthwise-separable convolutions}~\cite{howard2017}: a standard $3\!\times\!3$ convolution ($C_\text{in}\!\times\!C_\text{out}\!\times\!9$ params) is factored into a depthwise filter ($C_\text{in}\!\times\!9$) followed by a pointwise $1\!\times\!1$ filter ($C_\text{in}\!\times\!C_\text{out}$), yielding $\sim$8.9$\times$ fewer parameters. Architecture: stride-2 stem ($3\!\to\!32$), four DWSep blocks expanding to 256 channels with MaxPool, global average pooling (GAP)~\cite{lin2013}, and Dropout(0.5) + Linear(256, 3). GELU activation~\cite{hendrycks2016} provides smoother gradient flow than ReLU. GAP eliminates FC layers, providing parameter reduction and implicit spatial regularisation.

\subsection{Existing CNN: EfficientNet-B3}

EfficientNet-B3~\cite{tan2019} applies compound scaling---jointly increasing depth, width, and resolution---more principled than scaling one dimension alone. Its native $300\!\times\!300$ input matches preprocessing exactly. Feature dimension is 1536, with $\sim$12M total parameters. Three training strategies are evaluated:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Scratch}: Random initialisation, all parameters trainable, LR $10^{-3}$.
    \item \textbf{Feature extraction}: ImageNet backbone frozen; only head trains (LR $10^{-3}$).
    \item \textbf{Fine-tuning}: Early blocks (\texttt{features[0:5]}) frozen; upper backbone LR $10^{-4}$, head LR $10^{-3}$---differential learning rates \cite{howard2018} prevent catastrophic forgetting while enabling domain adaptation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Training Components}

\textbf{Loss}: CrossEntropyLoss with conditional label smoothing. \textbf{Optimiser}: AdamW~\cite{loshchilov2019} with decoupled weight decay $10^{-4}$. \textbf{Gradient clipping}: $\|\nabla\|_\text{max}\!=\!1.0$, stabilising fine-tuning~\cite{pascanu2013}. \textbf{Scheduler}: CosineAnnealingLR ($T_\text{max}\!=\!20$, $\eta_\text{min}\!=\!10^{-6}$). \textbf{Early stopping}: patience 10 on validation loss, restoring best checkpoint.

The custom LightXRayCNN achieves respectable 0.8686 with only $\sim$1.2M parameters---competitive given it trains from scratch.

\section{Final Test-Set Evaluation}

The best model (EfficientNet-B3, fine-tune) was retrained on the full 80\% training pool (2{,}502 train / 278 internal holdout for early stopping) and evaluated \emph{once} on the locked test set using \textbf{test-time augmentation} (TTA): five inference passes (original, horizontal flip, $\pm5°$ rotation, mild brightness shift) with softmax probabilities averaged before argmax~\cite{shiri2024}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Test-set metrics. Three primary metrics capture genuinely different performance aspects.}
\label{tab:test}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{6.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} & \textbf{Aspect Captured} \\
\midrule
Macro-F1         & 0.9478 & Classification quality (equal class weight) \\
Cohen's $\kappa$ & 0.9200 & Agreement beyond chance (immune to class-frequency bias) \\
Macro AUC-ROC    & 0.9854 & Ranking \& calibration quality (threshold-free) \\
\midrule
Accuracy (ref.)  & 0.9468 & Overall correctness (majority-class dominated) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\midrule

Macro-F1 (0.9478) provides balanced classification quality across all three classes. Cohen's $\kappa$ (0.9200) confirms performance far above chance---a majority-class predictor achieves $\kappa \approx 0$. AUC-ROC (0.9854) evaluates ranking quality from raw softmax probabilities, orthogonal to hard-prediction metrics. Pneumonia achieves near-perfect F1 (0.9909), while Normal--Opacity confusion is the primary error source, reflecting their clinical similarity. Only 2 of 220 Pneumonia cases (0.9\%) were misclassified as Normal---critical clinically as this represents a missed diagnosis.
