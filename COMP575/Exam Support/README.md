## What are Genetic Algorithms?
Genetic algorithms are search heuristics inspired by Charles Darwin's theory of natural evolution. They are used to generate high-quality solutions to optimization and search problems by relying on biologically inspired operators such as:

* **Selection:** Choosing the fittest individuals from a population.
* **Crossover (Recombination):** Combining parts of two parents to create offspring.
* **Mutation:** Introducing random changes to maintain genetic diversity.
